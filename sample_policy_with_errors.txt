
**COMPANY HR POLICY HANDBOOK - 2024**

**1. Working Hours & Overtime**
- Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Saturday.
- Overtime will be paid at the normal hourly rate (1x) for any work beyond 48 hours.
  *(This is a violation: Factories Act mandates 2x overtime pay)*

**2. Leave Policy**
- Employees are entitled to 12 days of Earned Leave (EL) per year.
  *(This is a violation: EL is typically 1 day for every 20 days worked ~ 15-18 days)*
- Unused leaves will lapse at the end of the year and cannot be encashed.
  *(Potential violation: Encashment or carry-forward is usually required)*

**3. Sexual Harassment**
- Any complaints regarding harassment should be emailed directly to the CEO.
  *(Violation: POSH Act requires an Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) with an external member)*
- No formal committee is currently established as we have less than 50 employees.
  *(Violation: ICC is mandatory for 10+ employees)*

**4. Gratuity**
- Gratuity is payable only after completion of 7 years of continuous service.
  *(Violation: Payment of Gratuity Act mandates 5 years)*

**5. Notice Period**
- The company reserves the right to terminate employment immediately without notice or pay in lieu of notice.
  *(Violation: Shops & Est. Act typically requires 30 days notice or pay)*
